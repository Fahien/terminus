{#- Resolves `setting` from the page, its section, that section's ancestors and -#} {#- finally the site config. Tera V2 components are hygienic, so everything the -#} {#- lookup inspects has to be handed in by the caller. -#} {%- component config.get(setting: string, page_extra, section_extra, ancestors, config_extra, default_global_value=false) -%} {%- for ancestor in ancestors | reverse -%} {%- set section_data = get_section(path=ancestor, metadata_only=true) -%} {%- if section_data is defined and section_data.extra is containing(pat=setting) -%} {%- set_global ancestor_setting_enabled = section_data.extra[setting] -%} {%- break -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {{- page_extra[setting] or section_extra[setting] or ancestor_setting_enabled or config_extra[setting] or default_global_value -}} {%- endcomponent config.get -%}
